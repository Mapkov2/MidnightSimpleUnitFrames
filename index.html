What's new in 7.0

Show healing-over-time effects on your pet and on party and raid pet frames. Personalize aura icons with Icon Skins and add independent gloss to health and power bars. Configure Breath, Fatigue, Feign Death, Battle Start and Group Countdown in the Castbar menu.

Upgrades from 6.5 carry over profiles and character assignments and create a recovery backup.

MSUF also supports WoW Classic Era, The Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary and Mists of Pandaria Classic. One package covers all five clients. The available frames depend on the game client.

Install from the ZIP

Get the packaged 7.0.1 ZIP from CurseForge and extract all three folders into Interface/AddOns for your WoW client. Restart the game, enable MSUF in the AddOns list, and type /msuf.
